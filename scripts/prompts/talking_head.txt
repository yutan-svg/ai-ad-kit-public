Vertical talking-head video, fixed locked-off camera, medium shot, no camera movement, single continuous take.
{PERSON} sits facing the camera and speaks directly to the viewer.
The basic posture does not change during the take; only the fingers shift slightly and the upper body sways subtly in rhythm with the speech, with natural blinking and small nods, calm confident expression.
Background: {BACKGROUND}, soft natural indoor lighting, photorealistic, 4K quality, shallow depth of field.

{GUIDE}
